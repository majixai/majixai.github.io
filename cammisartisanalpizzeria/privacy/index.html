Privacy Policy

Cammis's Artisanal Pizzeria · Last updated: November 7, 2026

This notice explains how information may be handled when you visit the Cammis's Artisanal Pizzeria website. It describes the website as currently provided; ordering, payment, and other services offered by third parties may have separate privacy practices.

Information this website handles

The pages currently provide restaurant and menu information. The sample cart on the checkout page operates in your browser and displays a demonstration confirmation; it does not submit an order or payment to the restaurant through this website. Do not enter sensitive personal or payment information on these pages.

The website does not currently provide an account, contact form, or newsletter signup. The web hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and request time to deliver and protect the site. Consult the hosting provider's privacy information for details about its processing and retention.

Third-party ordering services

If you follow a link to a delivery, ordering, or payment service (including DoorDash), that service handles information you provide under its own privacy policy. We do not control third-party websites or their practices. Review their notices before placing an order or sharing information.

Cookies and local storage

The current pages do not require an account or use cookies for a sign-in or checkout process. Your browser may have its own settings or extensions that affect data storage, and the hosting provider or linked third-party services may use technologies described in their own notices.

Children's privacy

This informational website is not intended to collect personal information from children. Because the site does not provide a data-submission form, please do not send personal information through any browser feature or third-party service on behalf of a child without a parent's or guardian's involvement.

Your choices and this notice

You can choose not to use linked third-party services and can manage browser privacy settings through your browser. This notice may be updated if the website's features or practices change; the date above indicates when it was last revised.
